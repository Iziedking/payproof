# Payproof brand kit

Status: logo approved by the owner on 2026-11-17. Colours, type scale,
spacing, favicon set and social images are still to come.

## The mark

Two folded halves that lock together around an empty square. One half is
what you were paid, the other is what the rule gives. When they fit, nothing
is left over and nothing is hidden in the middle. It does not use the letter P.

Each half is one strip bent at a right angle, drawn as two flat planes that
meet on a diagonal fold. No gradients, no shadows.

## Wordmark

Bricolage Grotesque Bold, letter spacing -2%. The font's licence is to be
confirmed from its primary source before the brand kit is final.

## Rules

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least one quarter of its width.
- Do not recolour the halves, swap their order, round the corners, add a
  shadow or set the mark on a busy background.
- The mark may stand alone. The wordmark always appears with the mark.
- Below 24 px, use the mark only.
