Were you paid what the rule says?

你拿到的钱，符合规则吗？

Some rules decide money: China's year-end bonus tax, Nigeria's 2025 pay tax, the split a mining pool pays its members. Payproof puts each rule on X Layer as a circuit that cannot be changed, so anyone can check their own number in about a minute, and a solver can test the rule for every possible input and show where it has traps.

有些规则直接决定你拿多少钱：中国的年终奖个税、尼日利亚 2025 年的工资税、矿池给成员的分成。Payproof 把每条规则以不可更改的电路形式放到 X Layer 上，任何人大约一分钟就能核对自己的数字，求解器还能检验规则在所有输入下的结果，找出其中的陷阱。
