Straight answers to the hard questions

Is this tax advice?

No. Payproof shows how a published rule computes your number and where the rule came from. For advice, ask a tax practitioner.

Everyone

Do my figures leave my device?

No. The rule is read from X Layer and run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or saved on a server.

Privacy

Is it live?

Not yet. Payproof is being built for the X Layer TapeOut Accelerate hackathon. Everything on this page marked sample is not real data.

Status
Who can change a rule?

Nobody can edit a rule once its circuit is taped out on X Layer. A new law means a new circuit, published next to the old one, so past results still point to the rule they ran on.

What does a trap mean?

A range where earning more leaves you with less. Under the China bonus rule, every bonus from ¥36,000.01 to ¥38,566.66 keeps less than a bonus of exactly ¥36,000.

Who pays the network fee?

Checking your own number only reads the chain. Publishing a rule or paying out a pool round is an X Layer transaction, and whoever sends it pays its fee.
