## 2. Official rule texts (task `p1-fetch-sources`)

`scripts/p1/fetch-sources.sh` downloaded the texts in a Debian container on 2026-11-21. It
hashed them and extracted their text with poppler-utils 22.12.0. The kept files are in
`docs/evidence/p1/sources/`; `SHA256SUMS` there also lists the PDFs and pages, which are not
kept in Git.

What the texts settle:

- **D1:** closed. The Fourth Schedule bands, read from the Act itself (page A540), are the six
  bands in PLAN section 6.
- **D11:** closed for the text. The State Council policy library copy of Announcement 2023 No. 30
  was read (not chinatax.gov.cn). It runs to 2027-12-31 and gives no rounding rule, so D12
  stays open.
- **D3:** narrowed, not closed. Section 162 (1) (t) exempts employment income where the person
  "earns gross income of national minimum wage or less". That supports a test on gross pay, at
  or below the minimum wage. Whether the test is monthly or annual is still open. The minimum
  wage figure (N70,000 a month) is not yet read from an official text.

## 3. Rule packs (task `p1-vectors`)

- `rules/ng-paye-nta2025/`, `rules/cn-bonus-2023-30/` and `rules/split-v1/` each have a
  `README.md` (the reading, its sources and open points), a `layout.json` and a `vectors.json`.
- `scripts/p1/make_vectors.py` derives the vectors with plain integer arithmetic, written apart
  from the P0 checker. Every vector says `"source": "derived"` and why it was chosen: each band
  or bracket edge with plus and minus one, the minimum-wage edge, the rent-relief cap, each
  deduction alone, invalid inputs, and the largest values the fields hold.
- Counts: 32, 22 and 23. No vector is from an official worked example yet; the READMEs say so.
- The script also asserts the figures quoted in the READMEs: N840,001 pays N6,000; 36,000 yuan
  pays 1,080 yuan; 36,001 yuan takes home less than 36,000 yuan; and the split example pays
  212,500. All passed.

## 4. Compiler core (task `p1-compiler-test`)

`compiler/src/netlist.ts` (decode, encode, load with the well-formedness checks, one beat,
`evaluate`, bit packing) and `compiler/src/layout.ts` (field `pack` and `unpack`). They are
written from the TAP-02 reference implementation, are TypeScript with no dependencies, and run
by Node 24.22.1 directly.

`node --test`, 26 tests, **26 passed** (two runs, 1.46 s and 1.86 s; the second is saved as `docs/evidence/p1/compiler-test.log`):

- The vectors file's SHA-256 is the one TAP-02 names.
- All 5 valid circuits: decode then encode returns the same bytes; truth tables and beats
  (state and outputs) match, including LATCH and REF with state.
- All 8 ill-formed netlists are refused with the reference's own reason.
- The packing edge cases and the NAND truth table pass.
- For each rule, the layout matches the P0 netlist, and every rule vector runs through
  `pack`, `evaluate` and `unpack` with the expected outputs. That is 32 vectors on `ng_paye`
  (8,482 gates), 22 on `cn_bonus` (3,573) and 23 on `split_check_modc_32` (41,128). The
  vectors come from separate Python arithmetic, so this also checks the netlists against the
  readings.
- 200 random pack/unpack round trips per layout, and refusal of values that do not fit or
  fields that overlap.

The files tested were compared by SHA-256 with the local copies after the run: identical.
